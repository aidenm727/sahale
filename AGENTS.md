# Maintaining the Sahale public showcase

- Keep this repository concise and technically grounded. Application source,
  tests, and deployment packaging are owned by the private `sahale-app`
  repository; independent infrastructure engineering belongs to `homelab`.
- Support technical claims with inspected source or tests. Distinguish
  implemented behavior, prototypes, executed verification, and owner-reported
  deployment observations. Do not invent screenshots, metrics, or roadmaps.
- Publish only reviewed summaries and sanitized assets. Do not read or expose
  secrets, authentication stores, personal records, internal addresses, or
  operational identifiers. Preserve unrelated working-tree files without
  reading or changing them.
- For authorized local changes, check the complete diff, relative links, and
  changed-path inventory. Do not introduce a mission tracker, generated-context
  pipeline, or governance process for maintaining this showcase.
- Staging, commits, pushes, publication, deployment, dependency/configuration
  changes, destructive actions, and external writes require explicit owner
  authorization for the action and target.
